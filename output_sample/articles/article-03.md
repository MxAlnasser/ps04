## What to do next

The value of a news pipeline is measured by the quality of the next question it gives a team. New information should connect to a service owner, a dependency inventory, an incident plan, or a decision that can be revisited. That makes monitoring an operational practice instead of passive awareness.

The recommended loop is simple: capture the signal, map it to the systems the team owns, check the supporting evidence, decide what changes now, and record what remains unknown. Repeating that loop helps teams avoid both panic and complacency.
