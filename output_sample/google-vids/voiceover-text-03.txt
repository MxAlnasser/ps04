Treat news monitoring as an ongoing loop: capture a new signal, connect it to the systems, owners, dependencies, and incident plans your team relies on, check the supporting evidence, decide what to change, and record what remains unknown. Revisit the decision as new information arrives so the watchlist guides useful engineering work without prompting panic or complacency. Keep reported facts separate from interpretation, since the evidence may not establish every downstream effect; treat the watchlist as a decision aid, not a prediction. Coverage depends on configured publishers, transcript quality, and a 30-day window, and repeated reporting does not necessarily mean independent confirmation. Focus on what you can verify and act on now, then adjust priorities as the picture changes.
