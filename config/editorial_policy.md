## Signal to Ship editorial policy

Select material for engineering leads and product managers who need practical security judgment, not a stream of headlines.

- **Relevance:** Prefer concrete changes affecting software delivery, dependencies, cloud systems, privacy, or incident response. Reject items that cannot produce a decision or useful question for this audience.
- **Freshness:** Use only material published in the run's 30-day window. Preserve publication and retrieval dates separately; never treat a refreshed page or replayed recording as a new development.
- **Credibility:** Assess the evidence for each claim. Prefer primary advisories, technical details, reproducible observations, and named practitioners. Reputation and model confidence are not evidence.
- **Novelty:** Treat syndicated announcements as one underlying claim. Favor information that changes what a reader could reasonably believe or do.
- **Perspective diversity:** Balance institutional advisories, commercial claims, independent analysis, and practitioner experience. State which perspectives are missing.
- **Uncertainty and conflicts:** Label reported facts, source claims, and our inferences separately. Preserve disagreement when the evidence supports it, disclose incentives, and defer unsupported claims rather than manufacture certainty.
- **Exclusions:** Exclude undated, stale, inaccessible, duplicated, purely promotional, or technically empty material. A recent discussion of an old event is eligible only when it adds verifiable new information.

The series must include evidence from feeds, YouTube, and podcasts, and each piece must connect at least three independent publishers where available.
